The formula

Staff cost = minutes ÷ 60 × hourly rate.
Batch cost = staff cost + additional tool cost.
Cost per checked result = batch cost ÷ checked-result count.

Fictional teaching example: 90 staff minutes at 30 per hour, plus 1 in tool cost, produces a batch cost of 46. With 10 checked results, that is 4.60 per checked result. This is arithmetic, not a customer result or measured AI performance.

Setup, ongoing maintenance, and the quality of your checking criteria need separate assessment. Zero checked results has no defined per-result cost. Unknown costs prevent a complete comparison.
