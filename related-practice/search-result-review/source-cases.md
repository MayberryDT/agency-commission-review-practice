# Fictional search-result cases

These cases are invented. They contain no destinations to open. For each, list what is known, what is unknown and the next action before reading the key.

## S1: an unexpected address

A CSR searches for a carrier's agent portal and opens a result. The page looks familiar, but its address differs from the portal address in the agency's records. It asks for a password and lists a support number.

What can the CSR establish from this note? How could they verify the correct route?

## S2: a password was entered

A producer says they opened a portal from a search result, entered a password and saw an error. They do not know whether the page belonged to the carrier or whether anything downloaded.

What is known and unknown? What should they report and to whom?
