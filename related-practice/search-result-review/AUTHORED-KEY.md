# Authored review key

Try the [cases](source-cases.md) first. These are discussion answers, not evidence of a real incident or a working security control.

## S1: an unexpected address

The address differs from the agency's record; the note does not establish whether the page is malicious or whether the carrier changed its portal. The familiar appearance and number on the page do not independently verify it.

Pause before entering credentials or downloading anything. Use the agency's verified portal address or an established carrier contact. If the address is uncertain, ask the person managing carrier access to confirm it through that contact. Follow the agency's reporting process if the page is suspicious. A stored address needs review when a carrier changes its portal; it does not guarantee a site is safe.

## S2: a password was entered

The producer reports opening a result, entering a password and seeing an error. Ownership of the page and any download remain unknown; neither a compromise nor safety is established by the note.

Promptly contact the agency's IT or security provider through a known route. If the agency doesn't have one, contact the manager responsible for security. Follow the incident process and report the approximate time, device, password entry, error and uncertainty about downloads. Preserve details as the process directs. Do not send the password or test the page by signing in again.
