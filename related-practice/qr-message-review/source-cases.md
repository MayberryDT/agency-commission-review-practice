# Fictional QR-message cases

All three cases are invented. There are no actual codes or destinations to open. Read each case, then complete the blank review sheet before looking at the authored key.

## Q1: an unexpected document

A message appears to come from a carrier contact. It says a renewal document is waiting and asks a producer to scan a QR code on their phone. The producer was not expecting the request. The message includes a phone number for questions.

What can you confirm from this note? What needs checking, and which route would you use?

## Q2: a setup you started

A staff member starts an agency-approved sign-in setup through the approved portal. The setup instructions show a QR code on the screen. The staff member recognizes the steps from the agency's instructions.

What makes this different from Q1? What should the staff member do if a later screen or request does not match those instructions?

## Q3: a scan already happened

A coworker says they scanned a QR code from an unexpected message with their personal phone. They do not know whether a page opened, whether they entered a password or approved a sign-in, or whether anything downloaded. They are also unsure whether a payment was submitted.

What is known? What must remain unknown? Who needs to hear about it, and what should the coworker report?
