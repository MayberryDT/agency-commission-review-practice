# Check an AI-prepared daily briefing

A small practice task for an agency team considering a daily AI briefing. Use the sample notes below without connecting an inbox, calendar or agency system. The records and draft are invented teaching material, prepared with AI assistance by Cirlet. They are not captured model output or customer results.

## Try it

The briefing is for **Monday, October 26, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. America/Chicago**. Every time below uses that timezone. A status is true only as of its source note. Your task is to prepare a staff work list, not make insurance decisions or contact anyone.

Read [the source notes](source-notes.md), then check [the deliberately flawed briefing](draft-to-review.md). For every draft line, record:

1. The source IDs that support it.
2. Whether it is current, superseded, missing evidence or outside the briefing window.
3. The corrected wording and the person who needs to decide the next action.

Keep [the separate authored key](AUTHORED-REVIEW.md) closed until you finish. There is no model score or accuracy benchmark.

## If you try an approved AI tool

Verify that the complete task and all seven source notes appear together in one submitted message before evaluating the response. An answer generated from only part of the notes cannot be compared fairly with the review key.

Supply only these sample notes and this task:

> Prepare a staff work list as of October 26, 2026, 8:30 a.m. America/Chicago. Use only the supplied notes. Cite the note IDs for every item. Preserve confirmed times, separate requests from accepted assignments, and mark missing completion evidence explicitly. Keep superseded and future items in separate sections. Do not send messages, change records, decide coverage or invent a priority rule. Ask a person to resolve any ambiguity.

Compare its actual response with the sources before opening the key. A polished summary does not establish that a task was completed, a colleague accepted it or a calendar is current. This pack does not test permissions, live connectors or production behavior.

## Why this task

A public agency discussion asked how to use AI beyond smoothing communication; a reply described daily briefings. That is self-reported use, not proof of reliability, savings, buyer identity or interest in Cirlet. This original exercise tests a narrow question: does a briefing preserve the current evidence when a meeting moves and assignments remain unconfirmed?
