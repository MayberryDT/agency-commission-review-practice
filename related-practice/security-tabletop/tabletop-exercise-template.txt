Original fictional discussion template | October 22, 2026
Canonical: https://aicybersecurityforinsuranceagencies.com/readiness-kit

Allow 15 minutes. No live account changes, real transfers, credentials or client records.
0–3 minutes: assign facilitator, reader, decision owner and note-taker roles.
Read: a familiar-looking vendor message changes payment instructions and supplies
its own callback number. It says the usual approver is unavailable.
3–10 minutes: discuss the trusted verification route, approval, backup,
escalation and approved decision-record location.
10–15 minutes: compare the answer guide and assign one follow-up.
